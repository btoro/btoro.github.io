Personal use

A private mail and calendar assistant.

Hermes Personal Gmail connects its owner's Google account to an owner-managed Hermes assistant for searching, reading, summarizing, organizing, and sending email, plus managing Google Calendar events at the owner’s request.

What the connection does

The assistant can search messages and retrieve selected messages or threads when helping its owner. Google access includes Gmail read, send, modification, and basic-settings permissions, together with the basic account identity needed to select the correct mailbox.

The assistant can send requested messages or replies and organize selected messages or conversations: create and rename custom labels, apply labels, archive, change read or starred status, move mail to Trash, and restore it. It can also create and remove filters, manage saved drafts, and change supported Gmail settings: language, vacation replies, POP, IMAP, and the primary sending identity’s name, reply-to address, and signature. Saving a draft does not send it. Sending or discarding a draft requires the owner’s request; Gmail permanently removes discarded drafts. Google restricts account-wide forwarding, delegation, and secondary sending-identity changes to Workspace administrators. Filters may forward to destinations the owner has already verified in Gmail. Immediate permanent deletion of received/sent messages and emptying Trash are not offered. Google automatically deletes mail left in Trash after 30 days. Personal credentials are kept in a separate store from the owner's Work account.

Calendar actions

The Calendar tools can list calendars, read events, create and edit events, cancel selected events, and respond to invitations. Calendar permissions cover event access and read-only calendar metadata used to select the correct calendar and timezone.

The assistant makes changes, adds attendees, sends event notifications, or responds to invitations when the owner requests it. These tools do not change calendar sharing or delete entire calendars.

Who it is for

This is a personal-use integration for its owner. This informational website does not offer public sign-up, connect to a mailbox, or display email content.

Your Google connection

Access can be revoked in Google Account connections. The privacy policy explains how the assistant handles Google data. Contact information for the operator is shown in the Google consent flow.
