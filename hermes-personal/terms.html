Terms of use

Hermes Personal Gmail is a private integration operated by its owner for the owner's personal Google account.

Permitted use

Use the integration only with an account you own and are authorized to access. It provides email reading and organization assistance, sends requested messages or replies, manages saved drafts, filters and supported Gmail settings, and manages Google Calendar events and invitation responses at the owner’s request. It is not a public email service and this website does not enroll other users.

Review assistant output

AI-generated summaries and answers can contain errors or omit context. Review the original email before relying on an answer or taking action, and check recipients and content before asking the assistant to send. For inbox changes, review the selection criteria and whether the action applies to individual messages or whole conversations. Filters apply to future matching messages; a forwarding filter can share those messages with its configured verified destination. Saving a draft does not send it, and discarding a draft is permanent. Vacation and mail-client settings can affect future messages and replies. Mail left in Trash is automatically deleted permanently by Google after 30 days. For calendar actions, review the intended calendar, date, timezone, attendees, and whether a change applies to one occurrence or an entire recurring series. The integration's availability depends on the owner-managed system and the Google and AI services it uses.

Access and changes

The owner may stop using or revoke the integration at any time through Google Account connections. Its configuration and these terms may be updated as the integration changes. Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Operator

Contact information for the operator is displayed in the Google consent flow. This integration is independently operated and is not provided or endorsed by Google.
