Data practices

Privacy policy

Hermes Personal Gmail is an owner-operated personal assistant integration. This policy describes its use of Google account, Gmail, and Calendar data.

Data accessed and its purpose

With the owner's consent, the integration accesses the owner's email address and account identity, and Gmail data permitted by the read-only scope. A separate send permission allows delivery of messages requested by the owner. That can include message and thread content, headers, attachments, and mailbox settings exposed by Gmail.

The assistant uses this access to provide the owner's requested email searches, reading assistance, summaries, and requested outgoing messages. The configured assistant tools can search Gmail, read individual messages or threads, and send messages or replies.

Calendar access includes calendar identifiers, names, access roles and timezones, plus event details such as titles, times, descriptions, locations, attendees, and invitation responses. It supports the owner’s requested scheduling, event changes, cancellations, and RSVP actions.

Processing and sharing

Selected email and calendar content and results may enter the Hermes conversation and be processed by the AI services configured by the owner to perform the requested task. Responses may be delivered through the owner's configured Hermes interface or messaging service. When the owner requests sending, the recipients, subject, and message content are submitted to Gmail for delivery to the chosen recipients. Requested Calendar changes are submitted to Google Calendar. When the owner requests invitations, guest notifications, or RSVP, event information or responses may be shared with the selected attendees or organizer. Those services process data under their own applicable policies.

The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or publish the mailbox on this website. Google data is used to provide the requested personal-assistant functions.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's private, encrypted credential store, separate from Work credentials. Credentials remain until removed or revoked. Conversation history, retrieved content, and generated summaries may be retained in the owner-managed Hermes environment until deleted by the owner. Any retention by configured AI or messaging services is governed by those services' policies and settings.

Control and removal

The owner can revoke the integration at any time through Google Account connections, remove its locally stored credentials, and delete retained conversation history in the owner-managed environment. Revocation stops future Google API access; it does not automatically erase content already included in past conversations.

This website and contact

This site contains app information and policies. It has no mailbox connection, sign-in form, advertising, or application analytics. The hosting provider may process ordinary website request information under its own policies. Contact the operator using the support address displayed in the Google consent flow.
