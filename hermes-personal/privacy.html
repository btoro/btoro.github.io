Data practices

Privacy policy

Hermes Personal Gmail is an owner-operated personal assistant integration. This policy describes its use of Google account and Gmail data.

Data accessed and its purpose

With the owner's consent, the integration accesses the owner's email address and account identity, and Gmail data permitted by the read-only scope. That can include message and thread content, headers, attachments, and mailbox settings exposed by Gmail.

The assistant uses this access to provide the owner's requested email searches, reading assistance, and summaries. The configured assistant tools are limited to searching Gmail and reading individual messages or threads.

Processing and sharing

Selected email content and results may enter the Hermes conversation and be processed by the AI services configured by the owner to perform the requested task. Responses may be delivered through the owner's configured Hermes interface or messaging service. Those services process data under their own applicable policies.

The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or publish the mailbox on this website. Google data is used to provide the requested personal-assistant functions.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's private, encrypted credential store, separate from Work credentials. Credentials remain until removed or revoked. Conversation history, retrieved content, and generated summaries may be retained in the owner-managed Hermes environment until deleted by the owner. Any retention by configured AI or messaging services is governed by those services' policies and settings.

Control and removal

The owner can revoke the integration at any time through Google Account connections, remove its locally stored credentials, and delete retained conversation history in the owner-managed environment. Revocation stops future Google API access; it does not automatically erase content already included in past conversations.

This website and contact

This site contains app information and policies. It has no mailbox connection, sign-in form, advertising, or application analytics. The hosting provider may process ordinary website request information under its own policies. Contact the operator using the support address displayed in the Google consent flow.
